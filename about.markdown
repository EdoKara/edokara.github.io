## Background

I'm from Bloomington, Indiana and have an environmental science background. I went to
IU Bloomington for a Bachelor's in Geography and a Master's in Environmental science.
I have since moved towards a more general software and data analysis expertise base.
I currently live in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. My academic focus was on Water Resources considered
broadly, including policy, economics, and technical lenses, with increasing focus towards
more technical understanding of ecological and engineered water systems.

## Career

My first gig was working for the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources as
part of an Environmental Impact Statement team. I currently work for the
Milwaukee Metropolitan Sewerage District as a data analyst.

A lot of my work centers around developing robust solutions for
data transformation, analysis, and understanding for my organization,
including pushing the frontiers of what is possible for us given our
collection infrastructure.
